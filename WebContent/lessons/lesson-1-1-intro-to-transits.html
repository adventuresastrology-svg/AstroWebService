The Living Sky vs Your Natal Chart

Lesson Overview

This lesson establishes the single most important concept in transit work. The difference between your natal chart and the current sky.

What Is Your Natal Chart?

Your natal chart is a map of the sky at the moment you were born. It does not change.

It is fixed. It is your foundation.

What Are Transits?

The sky kept moving after you were born.

Transits are the planets moving right now in real time.

They interact with your natal chart.

The Key Distinction

Natal Planets
Fixed at birth
Your foundation

Transiting Planets
Moving right now
The current conditions

The chart is the structure. Transits are what activates it.

Key Terms

Natal Chart Fixed birth chart
Transit Current planetary movement
Natal Planet Planet at birth
Transiting Planet Planet now

Before Next Lesson

Look at your chart and today's chart side by side. Notice the difference. That gap is where astrology becomes alive.
