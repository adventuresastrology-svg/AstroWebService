What You'll Learn

A transit is simple: A moving planet touches your natal planet. That's it.

Find a Transit in 3 Steps

  1. Step 1: Open your birth chart
  2. Step 2: Open today's chart
  3. Step 3: Do any planets line up? If yes = transit

Aspect Types

Easy (Trine/Sextile)
Smooth & Flowing

Hard (Square/Opposition)
Tension & Growth

Planet Speed Matters

Fast planets = Quick transits (Moon = days)

Slow planets = Long transits (Saturn = months)

Your Task

Find ONE transit happening right now in your chart. You don't need to interpret it. Just spot where a moving planet touches your natal chart.
