Lesson Overview

Now that you understand the difference between your natal chart and transits, let's apply it. Here's how transits actually affect your life.

The Core Concept

A transit occurs when a moving planet makes an aspect to your natal planet.

This is the moment when current planetary energy meets your natal foundation.

That's where the magic happens.

How to Spot a Transit

  • Take your natal chart position
  • Look at today's planetary positions
  • Check if they form an aspect (conjunction, square, trine, etc.)
  • If yes, you're experiencing that transit

Transit Aspects Matter

Harmonious Aspects
Trines & Sextiles
Flow. Support. Ease.

Challenging Aspects
Squares & Oppositions
Friction. Growth. Change.

The Transit Timeline

Exact Aspect The precise moment of influence
Orb The influence extends slightly before and after
Applying Approaching the exact aspect
Separating Moving away from the exact aspect

Why Planets Matter Differently

Fast-moving planets like the Moon create quick, emotional transits.

Slow planets like Saturn create long, transformative effects.

This determines how long a transit lasts and how intensely you feel it.
